# AeroVista 3D

A scenic autopilot flight tour over six alpine regions: the Swiss Alps, Grand Canyon, Mount Everest, the Norwegian Fjords, Yosemite, and the Cascades.

Open it here: [chrisvonderau.github.io/aerovista-3d](https://chrisvonderau.github.io/aerovista-3d/)

This is a single offline HTML file. It does not need a network connection after you have the file, so it also works from Mail or Files.

## Play

Tap **Begin Scenic Flight** (or press A / Space). Audio starts only after that.

- **Touch or mouse:** tap the terrain or the radar to divert. Drag to orbit in Orbit view. Scroll or pinch to zoom.
- **Xbox controller:** A begins and confirms, Y opens the Tour Director, D-pad moves the focus ring, B closes it, LB/RB change cameras, LT/RT change altitude, left stick steers, right stick orbits, X or a right-stick click goes to the next landmark, and Menu toggles Cinema Mode.
- **Keyboard:** Space begins, WASD or arrows steer, Q/E or PageUp/PageDown set altitude, C cycles cameras, Tab or M opens the menu, H toggles Cinema Mode.

Quality defaults to High on desktop and Medium on touch devices. You can switch High / Medium / Low in the Tour Director drawer.
